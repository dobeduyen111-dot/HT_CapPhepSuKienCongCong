## BCrypt.Net-Next 4.2.0

`BCrypt-Net-Next.dll` is the .NET Framework 4.6.2 assembly from the BCrypt.Net-Next 4.2.0 NuGet package. The project uses it to verify the BCrypt password hashes specified by the ViewParty SQL scripts.
